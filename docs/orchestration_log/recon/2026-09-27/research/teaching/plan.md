# Teaching research — operational plan (2026-09-27)

Rulings: trace docs/orchestration_log/history/2026-09-26/events.md, 2026-09-27T00:29 to 00:39 lines. Skill: orchestration:research-tree.

## Question

Which teaching and coaching methods build unaided, lasting skill in anyone learning CS-adjacent skills; which of them form the Pareto set over skill per hour, durability, transfer and sustainability; how each transfers onto an LLM substrate; and what edge an LLM has in teaching and coaching.

## Decided (owner handover 2026-09-26)

1. Tier 0: the owner's intake (trace) calibrates loosely; generalizability outranks it. The seed (seed.md) holds peer claims: surveyors never see it; Tier 3 tests its load-bearing claims against primary sources.
2. Tier 1 index, one agent per field: education research, elite coaching, expertise research, AI tutoring, other fields found by breadth expanders. Reviews, meta-analyses, primary studies, practitioner accounts, each with a link.
3. Tier 2 survey, one agent per need: practice design; feedback and diagnosis; struggle and when to intervene; retention, spacing and testing; transfer; motivation and sustainability, attention differences included (CS-specific methods sought); elite coach versus good tutor; transfer onto an LLM substrate and the LLM's own edge.
4. Grading: evidence kind (trial, meta-analysis, observed elite practice, small AI-tutor study, practitioner account) × strength × relevance to its subdomain and to CS-adjacent adults.
5. Snake-oil filter: a claim with no primary source, a vendor's claim about its own product, or a claim that failed replication enters only as a marked exclusion.
6. Saturation per need: close when fresh searches in every field stop changing that need's map; every search logged with query and hits; an auditor re-runs a random 10% of logged-empty searches; one miss sends the batch back.
7. Tier 3 verify load-bearing findings at the primary source; Tier 4 resolve contradictions; Tier 5 one synthesis: graded evidence map, Pareto set, LLM-substrate transfer, loose calibration note. No design.
8. Models: haiku for fetch and index; sonnet for surveys, verification, resolution; opus for synthesis.

## Validity threats

- Most evidence comes from children and undergraduates, math and physics, short horizons; relevance grades must carry that.
- LLM-tutor evidence is young and small; single studies stay single.
- Self-report and feeling of learning mislead; unaided, delayed outcomes weigh most.
