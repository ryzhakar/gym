## Seed claims the surveys never touched

Every row above marked "not independently checked" is, by definition, untouched by any survey round (t0–t2) — 27 of the 45 checkable citations, concentrated in:

- **§1 (target/proxies), entirely**: Bastani, Chi/Siler/Jeong, Person et al., Koli Calling log analysis — the whole "what's a valid proxy for durable learning" evidence base is absent from `sources.csv`.
- **§3 (diagnosis/misconceptions), entirely**: Crichton×2 (Rust ownership model — directly relevant to this project's own first subject), Mitton et al., the LLM-misconception-following paper, and the older-adults hypercorrection replication.
- **§5 (coaching), entirely**: Tharp & Gallimore's Wooden analysis — the only checkable coaching citation in the seed — was never surveyed; nor were the 4 recalled-only items (Chi et al. 2001, Chi & Wylie's ICAP, Koedinger & Aleven's assistance dilemma, Bjork's desirable difficulties), which the seed itself flagged as [R] (not retrieved even by the seed author).
- **§6 (struggle/intervention), mostly**: wheel-spinning (Beck & Gong, the decision-tree study, Gong & Beck/Botelho), bottom-out-hint modeling (Shih/Koedinger/Scheines), all three interruption-timing studies (Iqbal & Bailey, Parnin & Rugaber, the Tanaka counterpoint), and D'Mello's confusion paper (fetch blocked, not confirmed either way).
- **§7 (subject-agnostic core), entirely**: DataShop/LearnLab, Difficulty Factors Assessment, the redesigned-tutor result, KT-parameter retuning, and the JEDM unreliability finding — none of the knowledge-component/learning-curve literature that underpins the seed's "core owns the learner model" hypothesis was surveyed.

Two citations were checked and **contradict or complicate** the seed's own text, not just extend it: Lee et al. 2026 reports a **118%** median inflation from truncation, not the seed's "75%"; and Sinha & Kapur's actual four productive-failure mechanisms and its scaffolding discussion do not match the seed's paraphrase (the paper leans toward scaffolding *helping* young/low-prior-knowledge learners, where the seed says scaffolding "added no benefit").
