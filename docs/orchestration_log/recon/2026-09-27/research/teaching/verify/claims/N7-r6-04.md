# N7-r6-04 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"A real-time LLM copilot that fed live human tutors expert-modeled suggestions during sessions raised students' topic-mastery probability by 4 percentage points overall, and by up to 9 points for students of lower-rated tutors, in a preregistered RCT (900 tutors, 1,800 K-12 students)."
Source: 10.48550/arxiv.2410.03017 ("Tutor CoPilot: A Human-AI Approach for Scaling Real-Time Expertise")

## Primary text (quote, location)
Abstract/Intro: "This preregistered study presents the first randomized controlled trial of a Human-AI system in live tutoring... We conduct an intervention with 900 tutors and 1,800 K-12 students from Title I schools... Students whose tutors have access to Tutor CoPilot are 4 percentage points (p.p.) more likely to master topics... Notably, students of lower-rated tutors experienced the most benefit, with... up to 9 percentage points over the control" (p.1). Results confirmed in body (~line 412): "Students working with treatment tutors were 4 percentage points (p.p.) more likely to pass their [exit tickets]" (p<0.01).
