## Claim as surveyed
"In a randomized trial with ~900 tutors and ~1800 K-12 students, tutors given a real-time LLM copilot that surfaces suggestions during live tutoring sessions produced students 4 percentage points more likely to master a topic than control; the benefit was largest for students of lower-rated tutors (+9 p.p.)." Attributed to DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2410.03017.

## Primary text (quote, location)

> "...involving 900 tutors and 1,800 [K-12 students]... students working with tutors that have access to Tutor CoPilot are 4 percentage points (p.p.) more likely to master topics (p<0.01).
