## Claim as surveyed
"In a 140-participant RCT in programming education, fading a worked example by removing conceptually-central steps first (concept-oriented fading) produced significantly better near- AND far-transfer than fading by removing steps in fixed position order (position-oriented fading) — evidence that how a practice unit is faded/sequenced, not just whether examples are faded, determines transfer." (DOI 10.1007/s11409-023-09362-x)
