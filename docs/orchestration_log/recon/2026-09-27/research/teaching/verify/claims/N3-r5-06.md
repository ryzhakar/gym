# N3-r5-06 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
In a blinded RCT of surgical-suturing training, remote asynchronous e-feedback (video review plus written feedback) produced no significant difference in overall skill-improvement score versus standard face-to-face verbal feedback at a 2-day retest. DOI 10.1016/j.jsurg.2016.11.011 (Al-Jundi, Elsharif, Anderson, Chan, Beard & Nawaz, "Effect of e-feedback versus standard verbal feedback...", J. Surgical Education, 2016).

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text (submitted-manuscript PDF, via cache — Semantic Scholar route). Abstract/Results: "From a maximum achievable score of 20, there was significant improvement in the overall mean score on the second performance of the task (first performance mean 11.59, second performance mean 15.95; P = < 0.0001). There was no statistical difference in the overall mean improvement score between group 1 and group 2 (4.74 and 3.94, respectively; P = 0.4927)." Results section (repeats verbatim): "there was no statistical difference in the mean improvement score between group 1 and group 2 (4.74 and 3.94, respectively; P = 0.4927)."

## Design facts
Prospective, blinded RCT (assessor at re-test, investigator 3, "concealed to the randomization"). n=38 undergraduate medical students recruited, n=37 analysed (1 dropout). Group 1 = e-feedback (video-recorded, no trainer present, emailed pro-forma feedback same day); Group 2 = standard face-to-face verbal feedback immediately after direct observation. Task: skin suturing on a latex pad. Re-test: "returned 2 days later and performed the same skill again" — a 2-day delay-to-retest.

## Retraction check
Crossref `works/10.1016/j.jsurg.2016.11.011`: no `update-to`, no `relation`. Not retracted.

## Grade (S R O, changed from survey? why)
S=3, R=2, O=1 — unchanged from survey. Single RCT with control and blinding at outcome assessment (S3); medical students, a professional-skill course population, not self-directed CS-adjacent learners (R2); outcome at 2-day delay — short of the O=2 threshold (≥7 days), so O=1.
