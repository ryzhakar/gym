# N1-r1-10 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"Across 17 studies (484 surgical trainees) of technology-enhanced robotic-surgery skills training, zero measured transfer to real operative behavior (Kirkpatrick level 3) or patient outcomes (level 4); all evidence sat at simulator-measured learning (level 2) -- the transfer instrument this field would need does not yet exist in its own literature." (DOI 10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w)

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text fetched (Unpaywall → publisher PDF, CC-BY, `link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11701-026-03633-w.pdf`).

Results, p.4: "There were 484 total participants over the 17 studies... Mapped against the Kirkpatrick framework for evaluating training, sixteen studies reported level 2 (learning) outcomes... One single-arm study reported level 1 (reaction) data only... No study reported level 3 (transfer to operative behaviour) or level 4 (patient-level) outcomes."

Discussion, p.9: "the available evidence is concentrated at level 2 (learning — simulator scores, kinematic metrics, R-OSATS), with one study level 1 (learner reaction), and with no included study reporting level 3 (transfer to operative behaviour) or level 4 (patient outcomes); this limits extrapolation of the findings to clinical performance."

Numbers and mapping match the claim exactly.
