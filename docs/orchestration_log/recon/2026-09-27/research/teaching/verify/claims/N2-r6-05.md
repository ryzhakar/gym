## Claim as surveyed
"In a 3-week, 126-undergraduate, 4-condition randomized comparison of Python problem-solving practice, the combination of a FADED worked-out example with metacognitive scaffolding (FWM) outperformed traditional (unfaded) worked examples with or without scaffolding, and faded examples without scaffolding, on both problem-solving performance and self-regulation-skill measures -- the specific unit-fading-plus-scaffolding combination, not either ingredient alone, was the effective sequencing choice."
Source: 10.1177/07356331231174454

## Primary text (quote, location)

Over the course of 3 weeks... The results demonstrate that the provision of metacognitive scaffolding with faded WOE (FWM) is the most effective for problem-solving programming and self-regulation skills."
