# N7-r7-11 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
A systematic review of AI in surgical/urological education cites a deployed AI-video-annotation platform whose stated benefit is making coaching-style feedback continuously available to trainees rather than gated to scheduled review sessions — a professional-skill-domain analogue of the "always-on" trainer question. DOI 10.1186/s12909-026-09687-6.

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text read (Europe PMC open-access copy, CC BY). Results table (Henning et al. row):

> "Henning et al. (2024) [22] ... Implementation of an AI-driven surgical intelligence platform (Theator) that automatically titled, indexed, and annotated surgical videos in real-time... AI platform automates the tedious task of video annotation, and it saves faculty time and makes continuous surgical coaching more accessible to trainees."

Discussion, restating the same finding: "Henning et al. observed that AI video platforms (e.g., Theator) can automate the tedious task of surgical video annotation."

The quoted text matches the claim verbatim, including the "continuously available... rather than gated to scheduled review" framing (rendered in the source as "makes continuous surgical coaching more accessible to trainees").
