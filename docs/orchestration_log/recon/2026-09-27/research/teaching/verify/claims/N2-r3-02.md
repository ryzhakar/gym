# N2-r3-02 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"Conventional/drill-style instruction beat problem-based practice on short-term retention measured by standardized exams — the reverse ranking from the long-term outcome." (Strobel & van Barneveld 2009, DOI 10.7771/1541-5015.1046)

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text read (same source as N2-r3-01). Results and Discussion (p.53–54):

"For the Knowledge assessment category, measures of short-term knowledge acquisition and retention returned mixed results, but tended to favor traditional learning approaches. With assessments delivered immediately post-course (Albanese & Mitchell, 1993; Dochy et al., 2003), outcomes of knowledge measures such as NBME 1 ... multiple choice questions, progress assessments using 250 True/False questions favored the traditional learning approach (Newman, 2003, who only discusses NBME 1)."

p.54: "Standardized tests that measured knowledge of basic science focusing on short-term acquisition and retention (primarily the medical board exams in their different versions) favored the traditional approach across all studies."
