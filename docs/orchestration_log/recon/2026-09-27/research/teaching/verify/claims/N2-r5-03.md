# N2-r5-03 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
The same interleaved-practice-improves-transfer effect that is significant in controlled lab studies shrinks to small and statistically non-significant in applied/field training settings. DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1377122.

## Primary text (quote, location)
Abstract: "in laboratory studies, the medium effect size was statistically significant (SMD = 0.75), whereas, in applied studies, the effect size was small and statistically non-significant (SMD = 0.34)." Discussion (p.12) repeats this near-verbatim. Results, "Laboratory vs. field-based (applied) studies" (p.7): lab subgroup "medium SMD = 0.75 (95% CI: 0.26, 1.25; p = 0.004)"; applied subgroup "small SMD = 0.37 (95% CI: −0.02, 0.69; p = 0.06)."

**Caveat, reported as found**: the applied-studies SMD given in the abstract/Discussion (0.34) does not match the SMD given for the same analysis in the Results section (0.37) — a numeric inconsistency within the paper itself between its summary text and its own reported statistic. Both values are "small," both are non-significant (Results: p=0.06), so the claim's direction and significance conclusion hold under either number; the discrepancy is in the third decimal digit reported, not the finding.

## Design facts
Same meta-analysis, lab subgroup: 17 studies, 46 effect sizes, n=738 (121 older adults, 542 adults, 75 <18y). Applied subgroup: 17 studies, 40 effect sizes, n=683 (393 <18y, 290 adults). Sensitivity analysis (lab): removing 2 outlier outcomes drops pooled SMD to 0.62 (95% CI 0.24–1.01, p=0.002), still significant. Sensitivity analysis (applied): removing 2 outlier outcomes drops pooled SMD to a "negligible" 0.11 (95% CI −0.13–0.34, p=0.36).

## Retraction check
Same as N2-r5-01 — not retracted.

## Replications & critics
Same citing set as N2-r5-01. The paper's own Discussion (p.12) directly engages a prior critic, Al-Mustafa (1989, cited via Brady 2004), on the same lab-vs-applied question: "CI is a laboratory artifact... i.e., CI effect is conspicuous in labs but not in real life" — a pre-existing critical position the paper's own applied-vs-lab split corroborates.

## Status: VERIFIED
Direction and significance conclusion (lab significant, applied not) hold under both numbers reported in the source; the source's own internal SMD discrepancy (0.34 vs. 0.37 for the same applied-studies analysis) is disclosed above rather than silently resolved.
