(2025) second-order meta-analysis (DOI 10.1080/03075079.2025.2498084) lists "student satisfaction" among the outcome types its moderator analysis covers within an overall ES=0.60 pooled effect favoring PBL — directionally corroborating, though it does not isolate the satisfaction-only effect size in the retrievable abstract. No contradicting source found.
