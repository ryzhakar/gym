# N1-r4-02 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
Post-hoc analysis, same RCT: feedback made calibration on correctly-diagnosed cases worse than no-feedback (p=.013).

## Primary text (quote, location)
DOI 10.1007/s10459-021-10080-9, full text (PMC 8938348).

"The exploratory analysis of correct cases only, which would indicate the degree of underconfidence, showed a significant effect of condition, F(1, 67) = 6.55, p = 0.013, η p² = 0.09, with the feedback condition being more underconfident (M = −0.35) than the no-feedback condition (M = −0.25)." — Results, Table 4 discussion.

Abstract: "Post-hoc analyses showed that on correctly diagnosed cases … calibration in the feedback condition was less accurate than in the control condition, p = .013."
