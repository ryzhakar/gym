## Claim as surveyed
"Across three randomized controlled trials (N=1,222) on math and reading-comprehension tasks, brief (~10-minute) AI assistance caused participants to perform worse and skip more problems on a subsequent unassisted set than controls, even though the AI improved performance while available; the authors explicitly state they cannot speak to the durability of the effect because testing was immediate." (DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2604.04721)
