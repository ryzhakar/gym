# N1-r6-11 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
In a large RCT (n=2383) comparing AI tutoring, human tutoring and no-tutoring control on unaided GRE post-tests, one AI tutor matched human-tutor learning gains at roughly 918x lower cost per percentage point gained (USD 0.0052 vs USD 4.81) - but the authors state this measured only immediate gains and explicitly call for longitudinal retention follow-up, which this study (like most in this round) does not supply.

## Primary text (quote, location)
DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2609.28470 — *StudentBench: AI and human tutoring yield equivalent GRE learning gains* (arXiv preprint, 2026-09). Full text fetched.

Abstract/intro: "Verbal GRE questions across 2,383 human participants receiving AI tutoring, human tutoring, or no tutoring... We establish that AI tutoring is statistically equivalent to expert human tutoring for GRE learning gains (p=.015)..."

Cost section (p.~7): "one AI tutor achieved learning gains equivalent to human tutoring... at 918 times lower cost (USD 0.0052 for AI versus USD 4.81 for human, per percentage point gained)" — matches the survey's quote exactly, including the p-value context (the text also gives p=.044 for this specific tutor's equivalence test in one place, and p=.015 for the pooled AI-vs-human comparison; both appear in the source, applying to different comparisons).

Discussion / limitations: "measured immediate learning gains without evaluating whether they persist over months... We hope these findings encourage longitudinal studies of learning retention after AI tutoring."

## Design facts
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | RCT, students randomly assigned to AI tutoring / human tutoring / no-tutoring control (control watched unrelated educational videos) |
| n | 2,383 participants; 13 AI tutors per GRE section |
| Population | Adults taking GRE prep (self-directed test-prep, adjacent to but not identical to CS-professional skill acquisition) |

| Delay to test | Immediate post-test only; no delayed/retention measure — the paper itself flags this gap and calls for follow-up |
