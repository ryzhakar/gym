## DISCREPANCY — group-size denominators do not match the source
The claim states "13/32 SG vs 4/25 PR." The paper's actual cluster counts for the post-intervention project phase (§4.4) are:
- Implementers: nSG=14, nPR=11
- Understanding-driven: nSG=13, nPR=4
- Debuggers: nSG=2, nPR=8

Total SG = 14+13+2 = **29** (not 32). Total PR = 11+4+8 = **23** (not 25). Sum = 52, matching the abstract's "52 students using an unconstrained LLM." The claim's numerators (13, 4) are correct and verbatim from the source; the denominators (32, 25) are not found anywhere in the paper and do not match its reported totals — they appear to be a fabricated or miscalculated addition by whoever wrote the survey claim, since the paper never states group totals of 32 or 25 for this analysis.

## Status: REFUTED
The core inferential statistics the claim quotes — β=0.72 p=.01 (S3 interaction), χ2=8.14 p=.017, β=1.30 p=.025, and the numerators 13 (SG) and 4 (PR) — are all verbatim-accurate against the primary text. But the claim asserts group denominators ("13/32 SG vs 4/25 PR") that do not appear anywhere in the source and contradict its stated totals (13/29 SG, 4/23 PR). Per the verification rule that an inaccurate quoted number is not a verified citation, this claim as written is REFUTED on that specific figure; the underlying scientific finding (SG condition produces more understanding-driven transfer prompting, statistically significant) is independently well-supported by the same primary text and would be VERIFIED if the denominators were corrected to 29 and 23.
