# N6-r3-11 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"In a large-N observational study of commit timestamps across 86 open-source software projects (11,059 developers; cluster analysis on the 1,108 most active), roughly two-thirds of the most active developers cluster into regular 9-10am-to-6pm office-hour work patterns, while a distinct third cluster commits mostly from noon until midnight on both weekdays and weekends -- direct, population-matched (professional/OSS software developers) evidence of large real individual variation in when CS-adjacent skill-work happens, consistent with an irregular-bursts-toward-daily reality rather than a single normative schedule." (DOI 10.1145/3180155.3180193)

"These two clusters contain 66% of all developers."

Third cluster (verbatim): "Fig. 2 also shows a third cluster (blue) that does not follow the regular office hour pattern. Exact match to the claim's "distinct third cluster commits mostly from noon until midnight on both weekdays and weekends."

All numbers and the two/three-cluster structure in the claim match the primary text exactly.
