# N5-r3-13 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"Pre/post control-group trial of a Chinese vocational-education teacher-development programme (41 experimental vs. 42 control novice teachers): the programme 'had significant effects on two sub-scales of teachers' efficacy (i.e. classroom management and student engagement) and one sub-scale of professional engagement (i.e. planned persistence)' -- the only professional-engagement sub-scale to move was the one that names staying in the work over time." (DOI 10.1080/02607476.2022.2072713)

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text fetched: Wayback Machine capture of the Taylor & Francis full-article page (`web.archive.org/web/20220506071635/https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02607476.2022.2072713`, captured 2022-05-06, the article's own OA license is CC-BY-NC-ND per Unpaywall). Direct fetch of the live URL (curl, WebFetch) returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare block); the Wayback capture from shortly after publication has the full body.

Abstract (verbatim): "The self-efficacy and professional engagement of novice teachers were examined in the context of a teacher development programme (TDP) in the Chinese vocational education context. A pre- and post-test control group design was used. The experimental and control groups contained 41 and 42 novice teachers, respectively, who were mostly in their first year. Multivariate analysis of co-variance and paired samples t-tests showed that the TDP had significant effects on two sub-scales of teachers' efficacy (i.e. classroom management and student engagement) and one sub-scale of professional engagement (i.e. planned persistence)." — exact verbatim match to the claim's quoted sentence.

Results (§Results, "Professional engagement"): "The MANCOVA results showed that the condition had a positive effect on the post-test score for professional engagement (Wilk's λ(2, 78) = .914, p = .031, η2 = .086) with a significant difference between the experimental and control conditions on the sub-scale of planned persistence (F(1,82) = 7.248, p = .009, η2 = .084). No significant difference was found between the experimental and control conditions for planned effort (F(1,82) = .514, p = .476, η2 = .006)." Professional engagement was measured on exactly two sub-scales — planned effort and planned persistence — and planned persistence (defined in Method as "a teacher's will to remain in the teaching profession") was the only one of the two to show a significant between-group effect. This confirms the claim's second half exactly: planned persistence is indeed the only professional-engagement sub-scale that moved, and it is indeed "the one that names staying in the work over time" (Method, §Instruments: "planned persistence refers to a teacher's will to remain in the teaching profession").

Teacher-efficacy results (§Results, "Teacher efficacy"): significant for student engagement (F(1,82) = 15.703, p < .001, η2 = .168) and classroom management (F(1,82) = 14.007, p < .001, η2 = .152); not significant for instructional strategies (F(1,82) = 1.458, p = .231, η2 = .018) — matches the claim's "two sub-scales of teachers' efficacy (i.e. classroom management and student engagement)" exactly, with instructional strategies correctly excluded.

## Design facts

| field | value |
|---|---|
| design | quasi-experimental pre/post control-group design (not randomized): experimental group self-selected from TDP invitees (144 invited, 41 completed both tests); control group recruited by snowball sampling of non-participating novice VET teachers (42 completed both tests); MANCOVA (post-test as DV, pre-test as covariate) + paired-samples t-tests (Bonferroni-corrected) |
| n | 41 experimental, 42 control (83 total) |
| population | novice Chinese vocational-education (VET) teachers, <3 years' teaching experience, mostly first-year |
| instrument | Teachers' Sense of Efficacy Scale (TSES, adapted/translated, 3 sub-scales: student engagement, instructional strategies, classroom management) and Professional Engagement and Career Development Aspirations scale (PECDA, 2 sub-scales: planned effort, planned persistence); both self-report Likert scales |
| delay-to-test | pre-test and post-test around a program spanning multiple training modules (lectures, professional-ethics module ~80h, teaching-practice module ~107h) — post-test administered at program's end, not a delayed retention check |
