# N7-r3-06 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"In a randomized field experiment in a graduate finance course, a pedagogically structured AI tutor produced larger gains than unrestricted consumer-AI access on the instructor's own course-final exam (all 86 randomized students, +2.6 of 100 points, same direction as the primary written-reasoning measure, where relational-quality answers rose from 8% to 49% in the tutored arm vs 8% to 27% in the holdout); voice interaction nearly doubled engagement and cost 2.8x more than text but produced learning statistically equivalent to text." (DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2609.23958)

## Design facts

| field | value |
|---|---|
| design | preregistered RCT, single graduate corporate-finance course of an online MBA; 86 students randomized between a structured tutor (grounded in course materials) and a holdout with unrestricted consumer-AI access; within the tutored arm, weekly voice/text modality alternated within-student |
| n | 86 randomized (all 86 have course-final/midterm/weekly-check data on file); weekly post-test analysis sample smaller (32 of tutor arm completing post-tests; 46-student subsample for one final-exam specification) |
| population | graduate (online MBA) students in a corporate-finance course — professional adult learners, not K-12 |
| instrument | (a) weekly post-tests: 3 items/week (2 MCQ + 1 SOLO-scored short answer), explicitly **"conversational, AI-administered knowledge checks"** (p.31, Discussion/Limitations) — i.e., assessed while the student is still interacting with AI, not an unaided test; (b) course final: instructor's own **take-home** final (p.30) — no statement anywhere in the paper that AI/tutor access was restricted during the take-home final or the weekly checks |
| delay-to-test | none stated beyond normal course pacing; final is administered "after the study" (immediate, no ≥7-day unaided retention test) |

## Status: VERIFIED

The claim accurately represents the primary source (all quoted numbers check out exactly), but the survey's O=1 grade is wrong and is corrected to O=0 here — this is a structured-AI-vs-unrestricted-AI comparison, not an AI-vs-no-AI unaided-skill measurement.
