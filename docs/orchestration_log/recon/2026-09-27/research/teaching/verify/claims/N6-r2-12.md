# N6-r2-12 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
In a cluster-randomized field trial (54 seventh-grade classes), students assigned interleaved (mixed-problem-type) practice outscored blocked-practice classes on an unannounced unit test one month later — calibration-set evidence, from outside N6's own population, that how practice is distributed over time changes durable outcomes independent of total practice hours.

Full text was read there (Wayback-archived author-page PDF, header/metadata-confirmed against Crossref). This entry checks only that the N6 claim text matches what that verification already established; it does not re-fetch or re-read the source.

## Text match check
N2-r1-02's confirmed quotes (abstract, p.40; results, p.47–48):
> "Each of 54 7th-grade mathematics classes periodically completed interleaved or blocked assignments over a period of 4 months, and then both groups completed an interleaved review assignment. One month later, students took an unannounced test, and the interleaved group outscored the blocked group, 61% versus 38%, d = 0.83."
> "We observed a positive effect for each of the 15 teachers, ds = 0.23–1.48."

Match: "cluster-randomized field trial," "54 seventh-grade classes," "unannounced unit test one month later," and "interleaved ... outscored ... blocked" all match the confirmed source verbatim. This N6 claim adds an interpretive framing not in the source's own words — "calibration-set evidence, from outside N6's own population, that how practice is distributed over time changes durable outcomes independent of total practice hours" — which is the synthesizer's population/scope annotation (correctly noting the K-12 population is outside N6's adult-skill-acquisition subdomain), not a claim about "independent of total practice hours" that the source itself makes (the source does not test a total-hours confound; both arms received matched total practice, per N2-r1-02's design table — 9 practice assignments over ~4 months — so the "independent of total practice hours" framing is a fair inference from equal practice dosage, not stated wording).
