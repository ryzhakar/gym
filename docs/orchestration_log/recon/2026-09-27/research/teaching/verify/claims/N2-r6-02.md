## Claim as surveyed
"In the same meta-analysis, project-based learning's point-estimate effect on math achievement did not reliably differ by education level (primary d=0.662, secondary d=0.467, university d=0.620; moderator test F(2,81)=0.95, not significant) -- i.e. this dataset gives no statistically reliable evidence that PBL's relative benefit over comparison instruction requires, or is degraded by, a particular level of prior schooling."
