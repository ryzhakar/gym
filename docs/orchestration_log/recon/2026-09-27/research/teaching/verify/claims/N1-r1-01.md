# N1-r1-01 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"Unaided/independent-performance testing, not assisted-task success, is the instrument that reveals whether AI help degraded genuine skill: an AI-assisted condition scored lower on an unaided test phase than a no-AI control across 3 randomized experiments." (DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2604.04721)

## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text fetched directly (arXiv PDF, `arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04721v4`; "Published as a conference paper at COLM 2026").

Results, p.4 (Experiment 1): "Participants in the AI condition had a lower solve rate (mean 0.57, s.d. 0.41) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.73, s.d. 0.34; t(305) = −3.64, P < 0.001; Cohen's d = −0.42, 95% CI [−0.66, −0.19]) ... The lower solve rate of participants in the AI condition indicates that AI assistance impairs unassisted task performance."

Results, p.5 (Experiment 2, replication): "Participants in the AI condition had a lower solve rate (mean 0.71, s.d. 0.36) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.77, s.d. 0.32; t(583) = −2.33, P = 0.020; Cohen's d = −0.19...)."

Results, p.8 (Experiment 3, reading comprehension): "Participants in the AI condition had a lower solve rate (mean 0.76, s.d. 0.34) than participants in the control condition (mean 0.89, s.d. 0.25; t(166) = −2.72, P = 0.007; Cohen's d = −0.42...). This replication suggests that reduced persistence is not an artifact of mathematical reasoning tasks but a general consequence of AI-assisted problem solving."

Three experiments, three conditions, same direction and pattern each time. Quote location and number match the claim exactly.

## Design facts

| field | value |
|---|---|
| design | 3 between-subjects RCTs in one paper (Exp 2 explicitly framed as replication of Exp 1 with confound fixes; Exp 3 as a domain-generalization replication) |
| n | Exp1: 354 recruited → 307 analyzed (185 AI / 122 control); Exp2: 667 → 585 (308/277); Exp3: 201 → 168 (85/83) |
| population | US-based adults recruited on Prolific (crowdworker sample, not a professional or CS-adjacent population) |
| instrument | solve rate + skip rate on 3 held-out unaided test items (fraction problems in Exp1/2; SAT-style reading-comprehension items in Exp3); AI sidebar removed without warning before the test items, participants explicitly told not to use AI or outside sources |
| delay-to-test | none — test phase immediately follows the AI-assisted/learning phase within the same ~13–15 minute session; no retention interval |
| preregistration | not stated anywhere in the paper or appendices checked |
