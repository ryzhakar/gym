## Replications & critics
Semantic Scholar citation list (300 of 338 citing works scanned, keyword-filtered on mastery-learning meta-analyses/reviews):
- Winget & Persky (2022), "A Practical Review of Mastery Learning," American Journal of Pharmaceutical Education, DOI 10.5688/ajpe8906 (open access) — "Evidence suggests students within a mastery learning model perform better academically than those in nonmastery learning models with moderate effect sizes." Directionally corroborates, no reversal.
- Başar, Aşkın Tekkol & Gelbal (2016), "The Effect of Mastery Learning Model on Students Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis Study," DOI 10.21031/epod.277891 (open access) — independent meta-analysis of 24 studies (dissertations/articles/oral presentations), all with positive effect sizes, random-effects pooled ES = 1.546 (very large), significant moderation by education level (large at primary/high-school), no moderation by subject field. Corroborates direction and the "stronger at earlier/weaker-student levels" pattern in Kulik et al.'s abstract, though with a much larger pooled magnitude from a smaller, more recent, non-English-restricted sample.
No source found reversing the direction of the mastery-learning effect; no critique or reanalysis specifically targeting this 1990 meta-analysis was found in the scanned subset.
