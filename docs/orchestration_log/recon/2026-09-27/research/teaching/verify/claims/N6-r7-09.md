## Claim as surveyed
A PRISMA-guided systematic review of 10 programs that taught coaches reflective, intrapersonal skills (as opposed to sport-specific knowledge) found self-monitoring of one's own behaviour was the single most frequently used behaviour-change technique across the identified programs -- though the review flags low-to-moderate reporting of these programs' internal and external validity.

The most frequently used BCT was self-monitoring of behaviour, and low-to-moderate information was reported regarding internal and external validity."
