# N7-r7-04 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
The same RCT found that ChatGPT-assisted learners improved essay scores more than comparison groups, but their knowledge gain and transfer to new material were not significantly different from the other conditions — assisted performance outpacing transfer.

## Primary text (quote, location)
DOI 10.1111/bjet.13544 — same source and access route as N7-r7-03 (arXiv:2412.09315 preprint text used; Wiley PDF blocked by a Cloudflare challenge).

Results, Dimension 1 (essay score improvement, p.16): "significant differences in score improvements (F=4.549, p=0.005, η²=0.108)... the score improvement in the AI group was significantly greater than in the other three groups" (vs. CN: mean diff=1.970, p-adj=0.037; vs. HE: 2.120, p-adj=0.025; vs. CL: 2.200, p-adj=0.012).

Results, Dimension 2 (knowledge gain, p.17): "no significant differences between the groups in terms of the pre-test score (F=1.294, p=0.281) and post-test score (F=0.913, p=0.438)... no significant differences in knowledge gain."

Results, Dimension 3 (knowledge transfer, p.17): "no significant differences between the four groups (F=0.019, p=0.996, η²=0.000)."

Discussion (p.18-19): "although the AI group's essay scores improved significantly, their knowledge transfer performance was no different from other groups... our findings showed that the AI group significantly improved the essay scores compared to other groups, but no significant differences were found among the groups in terms of knowledge gain or knowledge transfer."

## Design facts
Same study as N7-r7-03: N=117, 4-arm lab RCT (CN/AI/HE/CL), same-session pre/post/transfer testing.

## Retraction check
Same as N7-r7-03: no retraction.

## Replications & critics
Same search as N7-r7-03.

## Grade (S R O, changed from survey? why)
S=3, R=2, O=1 — unchanged. This claim is the most cleanly and completely confirmed of the two bjet.13544 claims: all three ANOVA results (essay score, knowledge gain, knowledge transfer) are quoted with exact F/p/η² values matching the survey's characterization precisely, including the specific claim that essay-score gains did not carry over to knowledge gain or transfer.
