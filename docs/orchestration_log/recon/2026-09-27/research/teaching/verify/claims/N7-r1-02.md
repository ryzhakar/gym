## Claim as surveyed

"A GPT-4 variant prompted to withhold direct answers (a guardrail) eliminated the unassisted-exam harm seen with ungated access in the same trial, scoring roughly -0.01 SD vs control." DOI 10.1073/pnas.2422633122. Surveyed S3 R1 O1.

## Design facts

Same trial as N7-r1-01: cluster-RCT (classroom-randomized), ~1,000 high-school students in Turkey, closed-book/closed-laptop unassisted exam immediately following the same 90-min session's practice phase, rubric-graded, robust SEs clustered by classroom.

Effect size: GPT Tutor exam coefficient -0.004 (out of 1) / control-arm exam SD 0.277 = -0.0144 SD ≈ claimed -0.01 SD; not statistically significant — consistent with "eliminated the unassisted-exam harm" (vs GPT Base's significant -0.195 SD deficit).
