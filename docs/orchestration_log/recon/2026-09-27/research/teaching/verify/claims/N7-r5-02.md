# N7-r5-02 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
"The human-instructed group reached its first significant within-group improvement one repetition earlier than the AI-tutored group (2nd rep vs 3rd rep)." (DOI 10.1093/bjs/znad241.142)

## Primary text (quote, location)
BJS conference abstract (SP11.14, 2023, abstract-only): "Compared with baseline, groups 2 and 3 significantly improved in performance by the third and second repetition, respectively (p<0.01 and p=0.01)."

Full peer-reviewed version of the same trial (Yilmaz, Tee et al., *Scientific Reports* 2024, `10.1038/s41598-024-65716-8`, full text fetched), Results, "Within-group learning curves":

"The ICEMS group outperformed their baseline in the third, fourth, and fifth tasks... The expert instruction group achieved a steep performance improvement in the composite-score where they outperformed their baseline performance in the second, third, and fourth tasks... after which they reached a plateau."

This confirms the claim exactly: human/expert-instruction group's first significant within-group gain at repetition 2; AI/ICEMS group's first significant within-group gain at repetition 3 — one repetition later, matching the abstract's own group-2 (AI, 3rd rep) vs group-3 (human, 2nd rep) figures.
