## Claim as surveyed
In a large observational dataset (n=7147 Year 7 students, 500 classes, 127 schools) on a traditional (non-LLM) mathematics intelligent tutoring system, the relationship between ITS usage amount and external standardised-test outcomes (NAPLAN) was non-linear and moderated by school socioeconomic status: "how much use is optimal" is not a single number but shifts with SES.
