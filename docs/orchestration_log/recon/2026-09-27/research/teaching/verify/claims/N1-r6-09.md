## Claim as surveyed
"In a preregistered N=1237 study, actual completion time did not differ between independent and AI-assisted task completion, yet participants both predicted AI would be faster and reported lower subjective effort with AI despite equal completion times - felt ease/speed is dissociated from measured performance, the same structural failure mode N1 names for 'felt learning.'"
Source: 10.48550/arxiv.2605.23177 ("Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction")

## Primary text (quote, location)
Abstract/Results: "We conducted a preregistered large-scale behavioral study (N = 1237) to characterize mismatches be[tween predicted and actual completion times]... While actual completion times between independent completion and AI-assisted completion did not differ, participants predicted AI to be significantly faster... [T]ime and effort dissociate: participants reported lower subjective effort with AI despite equivalent completion times." (p.1, abstract; full_text_read=Y, cached PDF)
Design detail (Results section, ~line 100): "Our sample size was N=1237 in total: 401 in the prediction sample and 836 in the completion sample" — recruited from an "adult population" (Prolific), between-subjects design used "to avoid carryover biases" (Discussion).
