## Replications & critics
Same citation scan as N2-r3-03. Wang, Zhang & Cheng (2026) three-level meta-analysis of math-only PBL studies (DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1868415) tested an overlapping-but-not-identical moderator set and found: "No significant moderators were found for educational level, class size, group size, mathematics knowledge domain, publication type, or the number of technology types used" — i.e., in their narrower domain, *technology-type* (their analogue to Chen & Yang's "IT support") was also non-significant, contradicting Chen & Yang's finding that IT support does moderate effect size. This is a partial contradiction on one specific moderator, confined to the math subdomain, using a different (three-level, outlier-trimmed) model. It corroborates the "educational stage/level not a significant moderator" half of the claim and "group size not significant" half, but weakens confidence in the IT-support moderator generalizing beyond Chen & Yang's own sample.
No source found disputing the subject-area or school-location moderator findings.
