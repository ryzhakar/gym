## Primary text (quote, location)
Full text fetched: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.23543v1` (pdftotext -layout).

Abstract (lines 21-28): "...an experiment involving on-demand AI assistance, where participants complete tasks before, during, and after AI is available. By experimentally varying AI request costs, we find that lower-cost assistance induces more frequent AI use. We also find that participants who request AI assistance during the AI-access phase perform worse at the task after assistance is removed, and their subsequent unassisted performance is overestimated when predicted from earlier AI-assisted performance. We use a Bayesian latent ability model to separate initial ability, post-AI ability, and participant-specific skill change..."

Key numeric result (lines 320-327): "for participants who did not request AI, Phase 3 performance was underestimated by 0.15 reward-rate units on average. In contrast, for AI users, Phase 3 performance was overestimated... performance of participants while using AI (in Phase 2) systematically overestimates their subsequent performance when conducting the task on their own without AI assistance (in Phase 3)."

## Design facts
- Design: three-phase (Phase 1 = pre-AI baseline, Phase 2 = AI-access with experimentally varied request cost: No-AI / Low-cost AI / High-cost AI, Phase 3 = post-AI unassisted) between-subjects randomized experiment on a logic-puzzle task (lines 187-207).
- n: recruited 150; excluded 26 (1 attention-check fail, 14 inattentive, 8 inconsistent self-reported/logged AI use, 2 time outliers, 1 near-zero-accuracy low-effort); final n=124 (42 No-AI, 43 Low-cost AI, 39 High-cost AI) (lines 216-238).
- Population: English-speaking US adults 18+ via Prolific, ≥undergraduate degree; mean age 40 (SD 12); 59 male/64 female/1 undisclosed. IRB-approved (UC Irvine #7206).
- Instrument: logic-puzzle reward rate (correct objects/minute); Bayesian latent-ability model (θ) fit to accuracy + log response time across phases.
- Delay-to-test: Phase 3 follows Phase 2 in the same session, separated only by short breaks — no ≥7-day delayed retest. O=1, not O=2.
