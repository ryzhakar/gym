# N1-r4-10 — Verification

## Claim as surveyed
Counterbalanced field quasi-experiment (396 undergrads, 2 courses): open-book/cheat-sheet/closed-book exams differed in assisted score, zero difference on an unannounced closed-book retention quiz 2 weeks later (Psychology F(2,105)=0.03, p>.05; Statistics t(184)=0.86, p>.05).

## Primary text (quote, location)
DOI 10.17265/2159-5542/2012.08.004, full text PDF (David Publisher, 10pp).

p.474, "Retention Quizzes": "As it can be seen in Table 1, there were no differences in retention quiz performance across exam types in either course: In Introductory Psychology, an ANOVA comparing quizzes after open-book, cheat sheet, and closed-book exams finds F(2, 105) = 0.03, p > 0.05; In Statistics, an independent samples t-test comparing quizzes given after open-book and cheat sheet exams finds t(184) = 0.86, p > 0.05."

p.472, Method: "Retention of material. Two weeks after the first two exams in five sections of Introductory Psychology and in all four sections of Statistics, an unannounced closed-book retention quiz was given. This quiz was anonymous."

p.473, Results: assisted exam scores did differ (Psychology ANOVA F(2,130)=7.74, p<.05, closed-book lower than open-book/cheat-sheet).

## Design facts

| field | value |
|---|---|
| design | counterbalanced field quasi-experiment across course sections |
| n | 396 undergraduates, small liberal-arts university, 2 courses (Intro Psychology, Statistics) |
| conditions | open-book, cheat-sheet, closed-book (Statistics: open-book/cheat-sheet only, no closed-book) |
| outcome measured | exam score (assisted) + unannounced closed-book retention quiz 2 weeks later (unaided, delayed) |
| instrument | course exams + retention quiz; also test-anxiety scale (Morris et al. 1981) |
