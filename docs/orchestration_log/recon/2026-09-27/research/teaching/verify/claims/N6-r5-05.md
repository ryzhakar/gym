## Claim as surveyed
A meta-analysis (17 between-person samples, N=2473; 14 within-person samples, N=12129) finds unfinished work tasks are reliably associated with more work-related thoughts (especially affective rumination) during off-job time, with a stronger relationship between people (rho=.382) than within the same person across days (rho=.247) — direct, quantitatively synthesized support for the Zeigarnik-effect mechanism (unfinished tasks keep pulling attention back) in a real work-recovery context, though the outcome is self-reported thought intrusion, not skill performance. DOI 10.1080/10615806.2026.2616302.
