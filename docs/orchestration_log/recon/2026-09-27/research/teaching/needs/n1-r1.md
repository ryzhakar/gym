## Disagreements between sources

- Self-report self-regulation constructs are called both "often miscalibrated" (Bjork, Dunlosky &
  Kornell 2013; Kornell & Bjork 2007, felt-learning framing) and a real, if weak, predictor of
  learning outcomes (Sitzmann & Ely 2011, 17% of variance, k=430) — not a contradiction so much as
  a magnitude dispute: self-report is worse than chance-free but far from sufficient as an N1
  instrument on its own.
- The power-law-of-practice vs. exponential-law-of-practice dispute (Newell & Rosenbloom 1981,
  already an index anchor, vs. Heathcote, Brown & Mewhort 2000, claim N1-r1-02) is already flagged
  in `index/index.md`'s own Disputes section for the expertise field; recorded here only because it
  bears directly on which curve-fitting instrument N1 should treat as valid for "skill per hour."
