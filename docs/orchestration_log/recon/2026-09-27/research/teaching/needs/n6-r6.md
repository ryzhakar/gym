## Disagreements between sources (pairs, one line)

- N6-r6-01/02 (VR RCT, daily vs. weekly) vs. N6-r6-04 (Cepeda spacing meta-analysis): the VR RCT's own daily/weekly comparison is a single concrete instance of the lag/retention-interval-ratio principle the meta-analysis states abstractly — not a disagreement, but flagged so a synthesizer does not double-count one as independent confirmation of the other; they are the same mechanism at two evidence grades.
- N6-r6-08/09 (Hupfeld et al. 2018; Grotewiel et al. 2022) on hyperfocus's relationship to flow: not a direct contradiction — Hupfeld measures hyperfocus prevalence alone; Grotewiel measures hyperfocus against flow in the same sample and finds them dissociated. Read together they suggest hyperfocus is common in ADHD adults but should not be assumed to be "flow by another name" when designing how to channel it.
