## Disagreements between sources (pairs, one line)

- Koriat (1997, practice-driven **underconfidence** in memory-task JOLs) vs. Kruger & Dunning (1999, low performers' **overconfidence** in ability-test self-estimates): both are miscalibration findings but point in opposite directions — different paradigms (repeated memorization practice vs. one-shot ability self-estimate by the least skilled) rather than a direct contradiction; a synthesizer should not treat "practice/skill improves self-assessment accuracy" as a single monotonic claim without naming which paradigm it comes from.
- Already recorded in the shared index (not re-litigated here): Bastani et al. 2025 vs. Kestin et al. 2025 disagree on net AI-tutoring effect direction, plausibly reconciled by guardrail design — the N1-r7-07/08 claims here are the "unguardrailed arm" half of that same dispute, viewed through the measurement lens (assisted-vs-unaided divergence) rather than the net-effect lens.
