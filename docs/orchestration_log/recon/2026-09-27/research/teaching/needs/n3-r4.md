## Disagreements between sources

- Mueller & Dweck (1998, N3-r4-03) vs. its two replications: the original 5th-grade lab finding
  (intelligence-praise undermines performance) did not replicate in 108 vocational-education
  adults (N3-r4-04, no effect on performance/goal choice), and replicated only on self-report
  measures — not performance — in 180 university students (N3-r4-05). The direction the field
  cites as settled breaks down once the population shifts from children to working adults and
  the outcome shifts from behavioral to self-report.
- Maniktala, Chi & Barnes 2022 (N3-r4-09): the adaptive intervention moved posttest *accuracy*
  (d=.45) but not posttest *time* — a within-study disagreement about which skill/hour dimension
  a proactive intervention actually buys, worth flagging against N3-r1's feedback-timing claims
  which treat "faster" and "better" as one outcome.
