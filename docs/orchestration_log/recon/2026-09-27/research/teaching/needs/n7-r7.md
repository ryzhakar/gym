## Disagreements between sources (pairs, one line)

- 10.1016/s2468-1253(25)00133-5 (continuous AI exposure erodes unaided skill) vs. 10.1186/s12909-026-09687-6's Henning et al. citation (continuous AI-mediated coaching access framed as a pure benefit): both are about "continuous AI presence" in a procedural-skill domain, but one measures a harm to unaided performance and the other reports adoption/engagement metrics only — not a true disagreement (different outcome types, O2 vs O0), but the pairing shows the same substrate feature (always-on AI) can be evidenced as either helpful or harmful depending on which outcome is actually measured.
