## Disagreements between sources

- `10.1007/s10459-021-10080-9` (feedback does not improve, and can slightly worsen, diagnostic calibration for
  residents on difficult cases) sits in tension with the more optimistic framing around scaffolding/tool
  interventions in `10.1152/advan.00156.2021` (faculty reported the MCC tool "improved the team process" and
  "improved their critical thinking") and `10.1007/s11409-021-09275-7` (collaboration reduced overconfidence
  bias). All three are self-report/small-sample studies in adjacent but not identical designs (feedback-only
  vs. a structured scaffolding tool vs. collaborative restructuring), so the honest read is "the specific
  intervention matters a great deal" rather than "feedback works" or "feedback doesn't work" — bare
  after-the-fact feedback (Kuhn et al.) underperforms a structural change to the task or the social setting
  (Garbayo et al.; Kolić-Vehovec et al.), not a contradiction so much as a moderator this round cannot resolve
  without the full texts.
