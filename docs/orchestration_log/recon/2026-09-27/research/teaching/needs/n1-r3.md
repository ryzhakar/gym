## Disagreements between sources

- CPR meta-analysis (N1-r3-01/03) vs. the school-CPR studies it aggregates: the review's own pooled continuous-metric result (non-significant decline) sits in tension with several individual included studies' large pass/fail drops — the review resolves this as an instrument-granularity artifact, not a substantive disagreement about whether skill decays, but it is a genuine disagreement about *how much* decay the same underlying data shows depending on the instrument.
- No cross-source empirical disagreement was found this round on the calibration-of-confidence sub-area specifically — the one calibration-training RCT located (N1-r3-11, null result) had no independent replication or contrasting study surfaced in this round's searches to compare against.
