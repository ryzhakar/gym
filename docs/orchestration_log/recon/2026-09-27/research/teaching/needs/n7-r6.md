## Disagreements between sources

- N7-r6-01/02 (Bastani et al., guardrail-free GPT harms unassisted exam performance) vs. N7-r6-08 (SSRN commentary disputing the RCT's confound control): a direct methodological critique pair, not resolved here.
- N7-r6-09 (Kosmyna et al., cognitive-debt/weaker-recall finding) vs. N7-r6-10 (Stanković et al. commentary disputing the EEG-connectivity inference and flagging small-sample power): a direct methodological critique pair, already flagged in index.md's Disputes section (ai-tutoring) from the earlier round that indexed both; this round independently reached the same pairing via a fresh search.
- N7-r6-11 (pre-LLM ITS baseline: mixed/often-null effects, 0.18–0.29 SD where positive) sits in tension with N7-r6-03/04/05 (LLM-era effect sizes 0.63–0.73, 4–9pp, 5.5pp): not a contradiction between two anchors on the same claim, but a scale mismatch worth flagging for Tier 4/synthesis — the newer numbers may look large partly because none of them have an independent replication yet (single-study inflation is a known pattern this project's own grading table (S3 vs S4) already anticipates).
