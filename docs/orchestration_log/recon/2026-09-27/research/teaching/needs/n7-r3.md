## Disagreements between sources

- CoMeT (N7-r3-01) frames "giving less away" (restrictive tutors) as failing to resolve the offloading problem and proposes a contingent middle path, while the Deskilling paper (N7-r3-02) and Liu et al. (N7-r3-03) find that a comparatively simple intervention — explicit metacognitive feedback about the cost of asking — moves the needle on its own, with no scaffolding-design change to the tutor itself. The three do not test each other's designs head-to-head, so this is a disagreement in proposed mechanism (design contingency vs. feedback-about-cost), not a contradicted result.
- The GCSE micro-RCT paper (N7-r3-07) treats its own g=0.33 effect as preliminary evidence for a rapid, iterative "micro-RCT" evaluation architecture, explicitly arguing that value lies in replication over time rather than in the single-round estimate; taken alone against the Nigeria RCT's larger, single-shot 0.31 SD estimate (N7-r3-11), the two studies were not designed to be compared to each other and use different outcome instruments (curriculum-aligned vs. curriculum + AI/digital-skills composite) — flagged so the synthesizer does not pool them.
