## Disagreements between sources

- N1-r6-05 vs. N1-r6-06 (same Cureus RCT): video-assisted instruction wins on immediate OSATS,
  SBML wins on 2-week retention — not a contradiction between two studies but the classic
  performance-vs-learning dissociation (cf. index anchor Soderstrom & Bjork 2015, already in the
  merged index) reproduced in a new procedural-skill domain. Flagged so a synthesizer does not
  read N1-r6-05 alone as "video wins."
- N1-r6-07's proxy-mislead framing depends on reading "no significant difference in knowledge
  gains" as a null result; the Cureus paper does not itself frame it that way, and n=30 (15/group)
  is underpowered to rule out a real difference — recorded as an interpretive addition by this
  round, not the source's own conclusion.
