- Within N4-r5-03: GPT-4's explanation-accuracy (96% correct) and its code-fixing rate (lower
  than novices) move in opposite directions in the same study — explaining a diagnosis correctly
  and acting on it correctly are shown as separable accuracy measures, not one thing.
- N4-r5-06/07 (this round) vs. EJ1325560 (already ledgered, N4-r3, a two-tier Rasch-validated

  three rounds (EJ1325560, EJ1105290, EJ1201190, EJ1360249) with no new mechanism between them —
