## Disagreements between sources

- N1-r5-01 (EEG classification, 91.83% binary accuracy for an objective, bias-resistant instrument)
  vs. N1-r5-04/05 (CAST framework's own transfer instrument is self-report belief) -- both are 2022+
  attempts at "objective-ish" skill measurement, but sit at opposite cost/rigor extremes for the same
  underlying problem (assessing skill without relying on interview/self-report), with no study
  bridging the two.
- N1-r5-11 (skill-specific durability pattern by prior familiarity, surgical) echoes N1-r1-06's
  durability-is-skill-specific finding (medical, tourniquet skills) from an earlier round, in a
  different sub-domain -- convergent, not disagreeing.
