## Disagreements between sources (pairs, one line)

- The Hidi/Renninger four-phase-model lineage (N6-r7-01, N6-r7-02) treats curiosity and interest as tightly coupled — curiosity as a trigger that folds into interest's developmental phases — while the empirical meta-analysis co-authored by some of the same researchers (N6-r7-03, N6-r7-04) finds only a moderate, highly heterogeneous correlation and an asymmetric relationship: the theory's assumed tight coupling is empirically looser and one-directional (curiosity leans on interest more than interest leans on curiosity), not a clean contradiction but a real tension between the theoretical model and its own authors' later empirical test.
