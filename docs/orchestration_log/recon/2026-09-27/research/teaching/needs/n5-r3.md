## Disagreements between sources (pairs, one line)

- None found between this round's sources. A nuance worth flagging for synthesis rather than a contradiction: N5-r3-10/11 (Jin & Shi 2022) show expert-feedback quality depends on subject-domain match between expert and novice, which complicates any reading of Lepper & Woolverton's "Wisdom of Practice" (already indexed, coaching #30) as describing one universal "master tutor" behavior set — the two are not opposed, but a synthesizer should not average them into a single undifferentiated "expert tutor style."
