## Disagreements between sources

- Bloom (1984, N3-r5-01) vs. Cohen, Kulik & Kulik (1982, N3-r5-02): both are the classic
  tutoring-effect-size pair the field cites together, and both are now tagged to N3 for the
  first time (previously indexed but never surveyed for this need, per n6-r2's own note). Their
  usual contrast -- Bloom's dramatic ~2 SD claim vs. Cohen/Kulik/Kulik's more moderate
  meta-analytic estimate -- could NOT be independently confirmed this round: neither abstract
  states an effect-size number. This round can report only that the pairing is unresolved by
  primary-text reading here, not that it disagrees by a specific margin -- reporting the gap
  in what was verified is itself the finding, per cargo-cult-science's "must not fool yourself."
- N3-r5-04/05 (handwritten > GPT-4 > stock compiler message, only 1/6 tasks) sits directly
  against the "generic/ungated LLM content underperforms a structured or human-authored
  comparator" pattern r3 already flagged (human-tutor hints vs ChatGPT hints, N3-r3-04) -- a
  third independent, convergent data point, not a contradiction. Flag for N7's synthesis.
- N3-r5-06 (feedback delivery *channel* doesn't matter for surgical-skill improvement) vs.
  N3-r5-04/05 (feedback delivery *content quality* matters enormously for debugging): together
  these suggest the medium (synchronous/live vs asynchronous/remote) and the content (expert-
  crafted vs generated) are separable variables with very different effect sizes on this axis --
  worth flagging to N7 since an LLM trainer is inherently asynchronous/text-mediated by default.
