## Disagreements between sources

- Lally et al. (2010) find a single missed practice day carries essentially no lasting cost to
  habit formation ("did not materially affect the habit formation process"). This sits in tension
  with the implicit design assumption behind streak-based gamification (a mechanic this round's
  `gamification streaks habit tracking app engagement retention` query went looking for direct
  evidence on, without success) that an unbroken chain is itself the thing worth protecting —
  Lally's data suggest the chain's unbrokenness matters much less than sustained overall
  consistency.
- Tabibian et al.'s adaptive, per-item recall-probability scheduling (`arxiv.org/abs/1712.01856`)
  implicitly argues against fixed calendar-regular review as an optimum, while the MOOC review
  (`heliyon.2023.e15220`) documents that many real courses are still instructor-paced on a fixed
  calendar — a live tension between what an optimization result recommends and what deployed
  systems for this population still do.
