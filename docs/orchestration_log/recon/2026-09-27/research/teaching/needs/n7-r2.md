  instruction-tuned assistant does).

## Scope

Only N7, round 2, as a lens over N1–N6's VERIFIED claims per plan line 192. This round did not re-read
the ai-tutoring field or re-run a fresh field survey; it read `ledger/claims-status.csv`, the 22
corresponding `verify/claims/*.md` files, `context/substrate-audit.md`, `resolve/ai-access-resolution.md`,
and the ai-tutoring section of `index/index.md`, then searched beyond all four for direct LLM-substrate
evidence. Of 22 VERIFIED N1–N6 claims: 7 hold (4 of those because the source is already an LLM-substrate
study, not extrapolation), 1 holds trivially (instrument-based, delivery-neutral), 2 gain, 3 fail (2 on
mechanism only, 1 on a delayed-outcome window), and 11 are untested with logged queries. No claim
transfers cleanly in the "flip to the opposite verdict" sense — where the substrate changes the answer,
it does so by making an old barrier (teacher retraining, a fixed test-delay instrument) irrelevant, or by
introducing a new default failure mode (revealing the answer) that human-taught studies never had to
rule out.
