## Disagreements between sources

- Third-party judgment vs. self-judgment as a proxy: Hoge & Coladarci's review (10.3102/00346543059003297)
  finds a *teacher's* judgment of a *student's* achievement generally valid; Kuhn et al.
  (10.1007/s10459-021-10080-9) and Meyer et al. (10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.10081) find a
  *professional's own* confidence in their *own* performance poorly calibrated, especially on hard cases —
  the "tutor impression" proxy and the "self-report" proxy the plan lists separately may not fail the same
  way or to the same degree, and this round's sources never test both proxies on the same task.
