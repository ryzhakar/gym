## Answer

The methods with a verified unaided outcome are v2's six (testing on held-out items with the assistant removed, spacing revisits to the retention target, interleaving problem types, subgoal-labeled worked examples for novices, a generation attempt before instruction that builds on it, problem-based practice for month-scale retention at an immediate-exam cost), and rounds 4–7 add three members: training the learner in an expert performer's measured gaze behaviour, which beat ordinary technical instruction in three small RCTs (n=20–30) and carried into competition over 10 rounds in elite golfers (N5-r6-06, 3 2 2); simulation-based mastery learning, behind video instruction immediately and ahead at 2 weeks (one RCT, n=30); and a tutor built from expert tutors' dialogue moves (K-12, n=34, d .71 immediate, .36 at 1–2 weeks against class only). Interleaving gains an S4 motor-skill meta-analysis (transfer SMD 0.55) whose benefit is significant in labs and small and non-significant in applied settings (0.34–0.37; retention −0.01 after sensitivity analysis), so its field evidence is still one K-12 cognitive RCT, re-found independently in round 7. Project-based learning is now verified on mathematics achievement (d 0.53, 0.28 after bias correction), and proactive hints timed from the learner's own hint history cut training time about 20% while raising posttest accuracy (d .45). Measurement adds three facts: assisted-exam differences vanished on a 2-week closed-book quiz, feedback on past diagnoses did not fix residents' calibration, and practice sharpened the resolution of judgments of learning while worsening their calibration. On an LLM substrate the replicated harm from handed-over solutions stands (all four trials re-verified), human-authored explanations beat GPT-4 explanations a second time (debugging time-to-fix, CS learners), self-explanation prompts added nothing on top of LLM-supported practice, LLM-aided revision raised essay scores without raising knowledge or transfer, and real-time AI coaching beat live expert instruction only on the machine score the AI is built on. Sustainability gets its first R=3 cell, a year-long gamified work portal for 50 software professionals, non-randomized, with a monitoring confound and outcomes that are counts and supervisor ratings rather than an unaided probe. Still no verified claim reports adults acquiring a CS-adjacent skill with a separately tested unaided outcome at ≥7 days: N2-r1-05/06 pool a 1-week assessment with two immediate ones, and N7-r3-04c's delayed outcome was LLM-assisted. Generated practice, continuous watching, always-on availability and assistant withdrawal still have no learning-outcome study, and the one data point on continuous AI exposure is an unaided decline (colonoscopy, −6.0 p.p. at 3 months) that stayed abstract-only. No need is saturated: round 8's recapture puts 42–71% of each need's title-relevant literature unseen, and the ledger holds 1.3–3.3% of what round 8 screened as relevant.

What was refuted here: N7-r3-04, N7-r3-05 (see "What was refuted").

Disputes carried from v2, and what moves them: `resolve/ai-access-resolution.md`, five conditions in order of weight: what the assistant did (answers on demand → harm; withholds → neutral; structured tutor vs active class → gain, not a no-AI comparison; tutor-facing → immediate gain, delayed null), how the learner used it (hint users ≈ control, answer users below; three studies agree, none randomized it), task type (harm where practice means producing the tested kind of solution; help where practice means taking in information tested by recall), timing (all harms O=1; at ≥7 days one working paper up, one abstract down), population (harm at every experience level in the R=3 sample; gain in a selective college, larger in upper ability quartiles). Round 3 adds a sixth, the comparator: the harm studies compare practice with an assistant against the same practice without it; N7-r3-07 and N7-r3-11 compare tutoring sessions added to schooling against business as usual, with the test unaided, and find gains; N7-r3-05c compares an AI tutor with a human tutor and with no tutoring and finds equivalence to the human. None of the three contradicts the harm finding, because none lets the assistant do the tested work in the learner's place. N7-r3-06 compares two assisted configurations and measures while assisted (O=0), so it bears on neither side. The GCSE and Nigeria estimates use different instruments and are not poolable (`needs/n7-r3.md`). Contingent support (N7-r3-01) and cost-of-offloading feedback (N7-r3-02) propose different mechanisms for the same offloading problem and were not tested against each other. Unresolved: guarded hint-only LLM vs no LLM on unaided skill at ≥7 days — no study; what happens after withdrawal — no study (N7-r3-10). Practitioner "vibe-coding erosion" cluster (`needs/breadth-r1.md`) and N7-r3-12 are self-report.

Rounds 4–7 disputes: D5 (self-explanation on LLM practice), D23 (AI vs human tutor), D36 (continuous AI presence: deskilling vs engagement), D37 (the Bastani harm vs a confound critique), D38 (cognitive-debt EEG study vs its critique), D39 (pre-LLM baseline vs LLM-era effect sizes), D40 (self-reported reflective engagement vs a controlled null), D41 (guardrail nudges). Three new results bear on the harm/gain split. An LLM writing aid raised essay scores and left knowledge gain and transfer flat, with fewer metacognitive processes (N7-r7-03/04): the product improved, the learner did not, and nothing got worse on the unaided measures. Self-explanation training and prompts, which help in human-run worked-example studies at abstract level, added nothing on top of LLM-supported practice (N7-r4-07/08). Expert-supervised LLM drafts gave +5.5 p.p. transfer over human tutoring, interval crossing zero (N7-r6-05, K-12). The set. Membership rule (this map's, stated so it can be checked): a method is a member when it has at least one verified, non-negative cell at O=2, or two or more non-negative cells; when that O=2 result is neither contradicted by another ledger claim nor carried by an interval that crosses zero; and when no other method has an equal-or-better verified cell on every axis where it has one.
