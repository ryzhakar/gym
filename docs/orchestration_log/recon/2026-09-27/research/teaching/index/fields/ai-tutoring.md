## Disputes

- #17 (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025 — ungated GPT-4 access harms unassisted exam performance) vs. #16 (Kestin et al., Sci Rep 2025 — a structured AI-tutor protocol outperforms active learning): both are RCTs on AI-assisted learning but disagree on net direction; the plausible moderator is guardrail/structure design, not "AI tutoring" as one undifferentiated treatment.
- #24 (Kosmyna et al. 2025 — EEG evidence of "cognitive debt" from ChatGPT-assisted writing) vs. #25 (Stanković et al. 2025 — comment disputing the EEG-connectivity inference and statistical basis of #24): a direct methodological critique pair on the same claim.
- #15 (Wu & Yu 2023 meta-analysis — pooled positive effect of AI chatbots on learning outcomes) vs. #17 (Bastani et al. 2025 — a single large RCT finding harm on unassisted outcomes specifically): the meta-analysis pools mostly assisted/immediate outcome measures (its own inclusion criteria are not O≥1 by this project's outcome-match rule), so the apparent disagreement may be an artifact of outcome-type, not a real contradiction — flagged for Tier 3/4, not resolved here.
