## Disputes

- **Ericsson (1993, 2004, 2019, 2020) vs. Macnamara/Hambrick/Oswald (2014) and Hambrick/Macnamara/Oswald (2020):** whether deliberate practice is the dominant driver of expert performance (Ericsson) or explains only a modest, domain-varying share of variance, smallest in sports and games (Macnamara et al.) — an active, multi-round dispute (#17–25), not resolved in this index.
- **VanLehn (2011) vs. Baker (2016) and Mackay (2001):** VanLehn's synthesis is read by some as showing human tutors' adaptivity/intelligence drives their advantage over less-adaptive systems; Baker and, earlier, Mackay argue the advantage is substantially explainable by simpler, non-intelligent mechanisms (engagement, pacing, availability) — a dispute over mechanism, not over whether human tutoring works (#28, #33, #34).
